# Visual Quality Rubric

Score each slide from 1 to 5 on each dimension.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative clarity | unclear | understandable | instantly clear |
| Hierarchy | flat | some emphasis | strong first/second/third read |
| Layout | cramped/misaligned | acceptable | precise and intentional |
| Style DNA | generic | recognizable | distinctive SOTA moves visible |
| Density | overloaded | usable | dense but readable |
| Finance tone | risky/casual | acceptable | professional and credible |
| Editability-aware design | screenshot-like | mixed | critical content native by design |

## Blocking Issues

- risk/source text unreadable
- title/body rasterized without approval
- no dominant element
- slide cannot be understood in 5 seconds
- decorative effect interferes with business content

## Acceptance Mechanism

A slide is acceptable when:

- no blocking issue exists,
- average score ≥4,
- Style DNA score ≥4 for cover/key pages,
- Finance tone score ≥4 for financial institution decks.
